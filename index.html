Cucumber with Beef Bowls

You get the best of both worlds with protein and greens!

A healthy and easy meal to make and prep.

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds of ground beef
  • Cucumbers
  • 1 bunch of green onion
  • 3 cups of instant white rice
  • Lawry's seasoned salt
  • Garlic powder
  • Onion powder
  • Red pepper flakes
  • 3 tablespoons of rice vinegar mild & sweet
  • 2 tablespoons of Soy Sauce

Steps

  1. Cook meat on stove top for 20 minutes, while it's cooking wash cucumbers and green onion then put them to the side.
  2. Add Lawry's garlic and onion powders to the meat. While it finishes cooking, add a pot to boil with 3 cups of water. When it's boiling, add 3 cups of rice then cover for 5 minutes to be ready.
  3. While waiting for the rice to cook, cut cucumbers and green onion into slices.
  4. Add the green onion to the meat and cover on low to keep warm. Add cucumbers into a bowl and add rice vinegar, soy sauce, and red pepper flakes. Shake it and all 3 items should be ready to serve and eat!
